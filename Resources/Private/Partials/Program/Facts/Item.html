One fact of a program. A category type fact lists the titles of its categories. A program field fact with "fact.isRichText" renders its value as HTML and marks it with "ce-bodytext" for the content styles of the site: job profile, performance scope and prerequisites, while their field has the rich text editor for the program page type. Any other value, credit points and a text field without the editor, is escaped, with its line breaks kept. Every fact but a rich text one keeps a plain span without class. "fact.identifier" is the identifier from the field list - a category type, or "creditPoints", "jobProfile", "performanceScope", "prerequisites".
